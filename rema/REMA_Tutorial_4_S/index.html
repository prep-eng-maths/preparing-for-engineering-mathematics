  3. In the hydrogen atom, the angular momentum \(p\) of the de Broglie wave is given by:

    \begin{equation*} p \Psi = -\bigg( \dfrac {jh}{2\pi }\bigg) \big( \pm jm\Psi \big) \end{equation*}

    Determine a simplified expression for \(p\).

    Solution:

    Divide both sides by \(\Psi \) to obtain a formula for \(p\) alone:

    \begin{equation*} p = -\bigg( \dfrac {jh}{2\pi }\bigg) \big( \pm jm\Psi \big)\cdot \dfrac {1}{\Psi } = -\bigg( \dfrac {jh}{2\pi }\bigg) \big( \pm jm\big) \end{equation*}

    We do not have any information about \(h\) and \(m\), but \(j\) is the imaginary number, and as it occurs twice we can simplify using the fact that \(j^{2}=-1\):
